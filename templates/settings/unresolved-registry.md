# Unresolved-Detail Registry & Delayed-Meaning Registry

Project-level ledger of open threads. Maintained by the **updater** at
archive time (from the chapter's cognitive state and reader review notes).
Entries are never deleted by agents; the author may retire them explicitly.

Rules:
1. This registry **prevents automatic closure** — a registered item must not
   be silently explained away by a later narration pass.
2. Registration is **not a promise** — an item may stay unresolved forever.
   Never let this become a checklist the plot must discharge.
3. Planner-level significance (`confirmed_significance`) must NEVER leak
   into a writing prompt as emphasis guidance — that is how automatic
   foreshadowing is born.
